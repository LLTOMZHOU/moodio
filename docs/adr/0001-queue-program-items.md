# Queue program items, not only music tracks

Moodio's Queue is an ordered program of Music items and Commentary items. A Commentary item may optionally be anchored as a lead-in to one upcoming Music item; it moves or is removed with that music item. Unanchored Commentary remains valid for a general transition. Commentary is optional editorial programming, not a required cadence; silence is normal when it would add nothing. The final DJ answer in an Active interaction is a Direct response and bypasses the Queue, while proactive commentary follows it so the Station can place it after normal Music completion and before the next Music item without interrupting music. A manual skip, pause/resume, playback failure, or other direct/recovery transport intervention suppresses waiting Commentary—including an anchored line when the Listener skips directly to its target—because the Listener chose the pacing or recovery must get music moving again. The DJ writes text; the Listener's Voice mode determines whether that text is also rendered as speech. With Voice mode off, a Commentary item is still shown and consumed at its natural transition, then the Station proceeds to the next Music item without waiting for audio.

A voice Direct response may briefly duck and resume Music only during an Active interaction initiated by the Listener. Background work never takes the audio channel; it creates a Commentary item instead.
